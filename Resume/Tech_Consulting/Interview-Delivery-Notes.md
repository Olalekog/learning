# Interview Delivery & Presence Notes

A running, self-observed checklist of physical delivery and presence
issues from mock/practice interview sessions — separate from the
content in [Behavioral.md](Behavioral.md) and
[STAR-Scenarios.md](STAR-Scenarios.md), which cover *what* to say.
This file tracks *how* it's being delivered, so it's worth a quick
re-read right before the next session. Add a new dated entry each time
a new pattern shows up, rather than editing old entries away — seeing
the same note recur across sessions is itself useful signal.

## Active checklist — review before the next session

Overall goal: improve physical presence and composure during interview
sessions.

- [ ] **Reduce unnecessary movement.** Still moving around too much
  while answering questions — stay more composed and use intentional
  gestures rather than continuous movement.
- [ ] **Improve posture.** Maintain an upright, relaxed posture
  throughout the interview; avoid leaning forward or downward for
  extended periods.
- [ ] **Maintain eye level.** Prevent head and eyes from being directed
  downward for too long — keep eyes primarily at camera level,
  especially while speaking, to create stronger engagement and
  confidence.
- [ ] **Sit back periodically.** Intentionally sit back in the chair at
  appropriate intervals to reset posture, appear more relaxed, and
  avoid gradually leaning toward the screen.
- [ ] **Improve facilitation and presence.** While answering, stay
  calm and deliberate — good posture, steady eye contact, controlled
  gestures, and an appropriate speaking pace.

**Practice goal**: Sit upright → shoulders relaxed → camera at eye
level → look at the camera while speaking → minimize unnecessary
movement → periodically sit back and reset posture.
